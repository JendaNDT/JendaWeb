## Relax prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square album cover artwork for Relax, part of an independent electronic musician's portfolio. Create a sophisticated dreamlike landscape, soft amber sun hovering just above still dark teal water, sculptural layered dunes curving into the water like gentle sound waves, distant mist and warm peach sky fading to deep ink. Quiet, tactile, atmospheric, cinematic composition, subtle analog grain and soft light, refined contemporary record sleeve rather than stock nature photography. Central visual strong at thumbnail size, warm burnt orange, cream, muted turquoise and near-black. Full bleed square artwork. No text, letters, logo, border, mockup or watermark. Artwork only; album typography is supplied in HTML.

## Celtic Code prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square album cover artwork for Celtic Code, part of an independent electronic musician's portfolio. Create a sophisticated sculptural Celtic endless knot made of aged dark green stone and brushed bronze, hovering over still dark water in a misty almost-black forest. The knot's interwoven strands contain very fine amber luminous seams, subtly evoking electronic signals. A soft pale green halo behind it, distant mountain silhouettes almost disappearing into mist. Atmospheric, tactile surreal art direction, cinematic soft light, restrained analog grain, refined contemporary record sleeve. Centered bold silhouette, muted emerald, bronze gold and near-black. No text, letters, logo, border, mockup or watermark. Full bleed square artwork only; album typography is supplied in HTML.
